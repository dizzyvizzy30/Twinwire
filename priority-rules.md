# 🚦 Altie's Priority Rules

Plain-English rules Altie reads to sort everything. Change a line here and Altie's behavior changes. No code needed.

## 🔴 NOW (today)
- Due within 48 hours
- Someone is waiting on me AND I see them soon
- Money or health at stake (bills, meds, appointments)
- Anything I pinned ("Hey Altie, this is top priority")

## 🟡 SOON (this week)
- Due within 7 days
- A promise to a person, with no meeting soon
- Follow-ups older than 3 days

## ⚪ PARKED (no guilt)
- No date, no person waiting
- Auto-hidden after 14 days untouched, never deleted

## 🔔 Nudges
- No daily cap. These rules decide what nudges me.
- 🔴 and 🟡 items can nudge. ⚪ Parked items never do.
- Rotate the cue style (photo, color, wording) so nudges don't go invisible.

## 🌦️ Mode limits
- 🚨 Survival: show only 1 🔴 item
- ⚖️ Steady: show up to 3 🔴 + the 🟡 list

## 🔁 Shifting priorities
- Say "Hey Altie, move X to now / later", or drag it
- My override always beats these rules
