## Brand Identity & Names

- **Twinwire (Primary):** A sleek, tech-forward name that emphasizes the invisible, unbreakable connection between your actual self and your digital memory thread.
- **Altie (Secondary / Wake Word):** Kept as a highly viable alternative or the approachable voice-assistant name within the app ("Hey Altie").

**Priorities:** 🔴 Now · 🟡 Soon · ⚪ Parked, sorted by plain-English rules in [priority-rules.md](priority-rules.md) that can be edited anytime.

**People tiers:** 💛 Inner · 🤝 Close · 👋 Good · 🌐 Known, moving up automatically as you interact, per [people-tier-rules.md](people-tier-rules.md).

## 🧠 Anti Brain Rot (design rules)

**Twinwire makes you sharper, not lazier. It gets you back to your life fast.**

🚫 **Never:**
- A feed or endless scroll: every screen ends with "✅ All caught up"
- Streaks, usage points, red badges or autoplay
- Measuring success by time spent in the app
- Writing your thoughts for you: you say it, Altie sorts it, and lessons stay in your words

✅ **Always:**
- **In and out:** a 30-second brief, a 3-second card. We measure minutes saved.
- **Recall before reveal, in calm moments only:** in the evening plan, "Tomorrow you see Priya. What do you remember?" before showing her card. Never when you need the answer fast.
- **Purpose on intake:** saving a link, video or tool asks "What for?" (one tap on a project chip). If it has no purpose after 14 days, it's parked.
- **Made over consumed:** shown kindly in the weekly mirror (U6)
- **The original is one tap away,** and AI summaries are labeled as summaries

⚙️ **🏋️ Brain workout setting:** Off · Light (default) · Full. The simpler mode for memory conditions starts at Off.

**Science notes:**
- Recalling before seeing the answer strengthens memory: the testing effect (Roediger & Karpicke 2006) and the generation effect (Slamecka & Graf 1978)
- Offloading helps in the moment but weakens memory of what was offloaded (Risko & Gilbert 2016)
- Early studies link letting AI do the thinking to less brain engagement and less critical thinking (Kosmyna et al. 2025, a small preprint; Lee et al. 2025)

## 🌀 Breaking the Loops

Fooling yourself · the consume-and-try wheel · procrastination · focusing on everything and nothing.

| Trap | Why it happens (science) | Layer 1: the mirror | Layer 2: a helper (later) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🎭 Fooling yourself ("I'm on it") | We plan from hopes, not from past data (the planning fallacy) | U6: said vs. did · guessed vs. took | "Your last 3 took 2× longer. Plan for that?" |
| 🐹 The consume-and-try wheel | Something new *feels* like progress | U6: took in vs. made · Tools: "signed up, never used" | "Finish one before trying a new one?" |
| ⏳ Procrastination | It's mood repair (dodging a bad feeling), not laziness | Stuck items shown kindly, with your last save point | Tiny first step · if-then plans · body-double timer · a self-forgiveness line |
| 🌪️ Everything and nothing | Open loops nag the mind, and switching leaves "attention residue" | 💡 Idea parking lot · Save points · Focus mode · an optional active-project limit (off by default) | "Pick today's one thing" |
| 🔀 Chasing 10 futures at once | A new idea pulls harder than the boring middle of a current one | U8: one thing for 30 days, with other ideas parked (not killed) until day 30 | "Day 30: another 30, or switch?" |
| ✨ Prep becomes procrastination ("not ready yet") | Learning feels like progress and carries no risk; perfectionism goes with putting things off | U8: your own done line ("works for 1 real person") · U6: made vs. shown | 🪜 Ship ladder, starting with the smallest step |
| 🙈 Hiding the work (posting, networking, asking) | A "no" hits harder; emotional swings are common with ADHD | U8: 💬 proof file (only real words people said) · 🎲 asks counter (counts asks, not yeses) | Post options (J12) · reach-out drafts (R5) |

**Science notes:**
- Sirois & Pychyl 2013: procrastination is mood repair
- Wohl et al. 2010: forgiving yourself lowers the next round of procrastination
- Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006: if-then plans help people follow through
- Buehler et al. 1994: the planning fallacy
- Masicampo & Baumeister 2011: making a plan quiets nagging unfinished goals
- Leroy & Glomb 2018: a "ready-to-resume" plan cuts attention residue, which backs our Save points
- Sirois, Molnar & Hirsch 2017: perfectionistic worry goes with more procrastination
- Shaw et al. 2014: emotional dysregulation is common in ADHD ("rejection sensitivity" is a popular term, not a diagnosis)

## 🧭 Detours, New Directions & Your Chapters (retrospect)

**Your collecting journey becomes a story you can look back on. Altie finds the patterns; you say what they mean.**

| | What Altie tracks | Example | When you see it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🎯 **Goal anchors** | Your 1–3 current goals (you set them) | "Ship XFIX" · "Build Twinwire v0" | Always, at the top of the mirror |
| 🧭 **Detours** | Each capture tagged on-goal or off-goal | "4 detours this week: 2 new tools, 1 idea, 1 event" | U6 weekly mirror |
| 🌱 **New direction, started without noticing** | A theme growing outside your goals, and its **first seed** | "'Memory apps' came up 23 times in 3 weeks. First seed: Nov 9, 'I forget things easily.'" | When the theme crosses a threshold |
| 📖 **Chapters + turning points** | Your timeline grouped into eras, with the moment each one turned | "Chapter: LensTalk → XFIX → Twinwire. Turning point: Nov 9." | U7 monthly look-back |

**Choices on a new direction:** [🎯 Make it a goal] [🔍 Just exploring] [🅿️ Park it]
**Detour outcomes:** 🔁 back on track · 🅿️ parked · 🌱 grew into something. Detours aren't failures; some become the main road.
**Meaning stays yours:** Altie shows the evidence (dates, counts, your quotes) and asks "What was this chapter about?" Your answer becomes the chapter title. Altie never names the meaning for you.
**Later: 📚 Your story as a book.** Chapters become a book with your titles, your quotes, photos and turning points, as a PDF or printed copy. You pick what goes in, and other people's private details stay out unless they agree. For people with memory conditions, this doubles as a life story book.

**Science notes:**
- Pennebaker 1997: expressive writing about experiences improves well-being
- McAdams & McLean 2013: making a coherent life story (narrative identity) is linked to well-being
- Di Stefano et al. 2014: about 15 minutes of reflection a day improved work performance

## 🛡️🌱 Two Hats: Survive and Grow

Altie borrows the tools a **therapist** gives you to survive and a **coach** gives you to grow, and carries them into every day. It never replaces either.

- 🛡️ **Survive hat (Survival Mode):** memory at the moment you need it, one tiniest step, breathing/grounding, no-guilt resets.
- 🌱 **Grow hat (Thriving Mode):** one goal with weekly check-ins, relationship follow-ups, lessons you kept, weekly wins.
- ⚖️ **Steady:** both, lightly.
- 🤝 **Bridge to real people:** catch therapist/coach homework and nudge at that moment; a 1-page "since last time" you choose to share; suggest a human after weeks in Survival.
- 🚫 **Hard lines:** never called therapy, never diagnoses, never reads emotions; crisis words show 988 and human help immediately.

## 🧩 Three Separate Spaces, Linked

- 🧠 **Memory:** people (with clickable LinkedIn / X / website links), moments, how you met, lessons.
- 📅 **Events:** found events, Calendar, who's going, your goals, prep notes.
- ✅ **To-dos:** promises, follow-ups, prep tasks, in their own list with their own Now / Soon / Parked.
- Each to-do remembers the event and person it came from, but lives in its own list.
- Profile links are only saved and opened. Altie never scrapes LinkedIn or X.

## 🎟️ The Event Journey (main use case)

- **Events home:** two big buttons: ➕ **Add an event** (say it · paste a link · snap a flyer) and 🧳 **Prep for one** (any event on your calendar, even ones Altie didn't find). Each upcoming event shows its prep state: Card ready · Prep 1/3 · Prep this?
- **Work accounts blocked by IT:** Altie only needs who + when, not email. (1) Read the phone's calendar if the work account syncs there. (2) A 20-second morning check-in: "Any work meetings today?" (3) Ask HR/IT to approve Twinwire as a workplace accommodation. Never auto-forward work email; it often breaks company policy.
- **Before:** J1 find events from your interests · J2 who's worth meeting · J3 your 1–3 goals · J4 prep the day before
- **At the event:** J5 quiet mode (only your top people) · J6 "Hey Altie, met Ravi, zoning lawyer" + scan their LinkedIn QR
- **After:** J7 reach-out drafts next morning · J8 "What do you want to keep from today?" · J9 follow-ups go to To-dos, linked back
- **Tools (the onboarding problem):** prep reads the event page for tools to set up (sponsors, sign-ups, installs, API keys) and makes setup to-dos · J10 log each new tool: what it's for, which project, which account, where the key lives (never the key itself) · a Tool library with "What did I use for…?" search and "free credits end in 5 days" alerts
- **J11 Save point:** as the event ends, "Where did you leave off? What's next?" (your words) + tools and people auto-collected → next time you open the project: "Resume XFIX? You left off at…"
- **J7 upgraded, who to message and why:** ranked by benefit to your goals. Each person gets: why them (matched to your story, e.g. "you both built agents"), what to send (a 2-line draft), and where (LinkedIn, WhatsApp or email). Draft only; you send it.
- **J12 Post options:** at the end of the event, 3 LinkedIn drafts to choose from: 💡 **Lesson** ("3 things I learned at…") · 🛠️ **Build** (what you made + tools used) · 🙏 **Thanks** (hosts, speakers, teammates). Each one suggests who to tag and why. You tap one, edit it, and Altie copies it and opens LinkedIn; **you post it**.
  - Built only from your own words, your lessons and your story. Altie never invents results or lessons.
  - Public facts only. Never anything someone told you privately. People you just met get tagged only after you ask them.
  - It matches your writing voice from a few past posts (paste them, or use the LinkedIn data export).
- **J13 Books suggested by people:** "Ravi said read *The Mom Test*" saves the book, who suggested it, where, and why. Status: 📥 Want · 📖 Reading · ✅ Done · ⚪ Parked (no guilt). The book shows on Ravi's card, so it becomes a conversation starter next time. When you finish it: "Tell Ravi you read it?" Its lessons can feed a post.
- **🪪 Your story inputs:** resumes over the years (each version adds to one timeline of roles), your experience, projects you've built (with GitHub or demo links), and all your profile links (LinkedIn, X, GitHub, site). Altie uses them to pick who's worth meeting, write "why them," and keep your posts credible.

## 👆 Tap Anything: One Sheet, One Next Step

Any bead (event, task, person, memory) rises into the same sheet, with the wire still visible above:
1. What it is (face or icon first) · 2. Why it matters (one line) · 3. ONE big next step · 4. Four quick actions: Later · Priority (Tier for people) · Open · Ask Altie · 5. Connected people, events, to-dos and memories.
- Next step by type: Event → **Prep now · 1 min** · Task → **Start 10-min focus** · Person → **Send a quick update**
- Focus mode: a timer ring, one step at a time, "Altie is here with you," "Stuck? Make it smaller," and "Stop for now" (it stays in Now, no guilt).
- Swipe right: done · Swipe left: later · Hold: ask Altie.

## 🔓 Log In Once

- Twinwire opens with Face ID or a fingerprint. No passwords to remember.
- Sites (Luma, Eventbrite…) stay logged in inside Twinwire's browser; you log in once.
- Calendars stay connected. During the MVP test, Google asks again about every 7 days; a published app doesn't.
- If a site logs you out, Altie asks once: "Log in to luma.com again?"

## 🔑 Site Access: Let Altie Act for You (later)

- **The fast path (saves time):** share an event link → Altie's 10-second summary (what, fit for you, approval needed, who gets your data) + "What Altie will send" → one tap **Register me** → Altie fills and submits, shows what it did, and adds it to your calendar when approved. If a site asks for a CAPTCHA, a code or payment, Altie stops: "Finish in 1 tap."
- LinkedIn stays Look-only, because its rules ban automated actions.
- One tap per site ("Let Altie use luma.com") with 4 levels: 🔒 Off · 👀 Look (read pages, find events) · ✍️ Fill (fills forms, you tap the final button; the default) · ✅ Act (submits free RSVPs on its own, within your limits).
- Hard limits on every site: never pays without your tap, never creates accounts, never posts publicly, never bypasses CAPTCHAs or queues.
- You log in yourself inside Twinwire's own in-app browser, so the session stays on your phone. Altie never sees or stores passwords. On a laptop, a Twinwire browser extension does the same.
- Activity log of every action; revoke any site in one tap.
- Safety: web pages can contain hidden instructions meant to trick AI agents. Altie only does the one task you asked for and treats page text as data.

## 🚪 Many Doors In, One Place Out

**Input (any door):** 🎙️ in-app voice / widget / Action Button · 📤 share sheet (photos, screenshots, links) · 💬 WhatsApp chat to "Altie" (input only) · 📅 Calendar (automatic: the phone's calendar covers Google, Outlook/Microsoft 365 and iCloud in one permission; Microsoft Graph API as backup for work accounts that don't sync to the phone)

**Output (one place, the app):** 🗓️ Memory Lane timeline · 👥 People faces · 🔍 Ask bar with answers that link back to the original memory

WhatsApp only replies with an optional one-line "✅ Saved."

**Save first, think second (every capture):**
1. ⚡ **Instant, on the phone, works offline:** the original (photo, voice, text, card) is saved with the time, place and the event you're at. Altie shows "✅ Got it."
2. 🔄 **Seconds later, in the background:** voice becomes text and photos or cards are read on the phone. Then Altie finds who, what and when, stores the contact, links it to the event and to-dos, and makes it searchable.
3. 👀 **One tap, only when unsure:** "New person: Priya Shah, Acme?" [✅] [✏️]. If the name matches someone already saved: "Same Priya from Oct?" [Merge] [New].
- With no internet, step 2 waits and runs later. A capture is never lost.

**Offline:** capturing, saved memories, keyword search, calendar, scheduled nudges, and reading photos and voice on the phone all work without internet. On phones with Apple Intelligence, sorting also works offline: facts, links and short summaries, done by Apple's on-device model. The rest needs internet: answers across many memories, briefs, web and event search, WhatsApp, backup and Register me. **Event pack:** the night before an event, while online, Altie downloads the people cards, prep list, tools list and the morning brief, so they still work on bad venue wifi.
- Tokens are only made when text is sent to the AI. At save time we keep three things: the original, the facts pulled from it, and an embedding (numbers that let search match by meaning).

## ⏱️ No Extra Time to Journal

- 📅 **Day-1 backfill:** past Calendar fills the brain instantly (who, when, how often), so cards work before you mention anything.
- 💬 **After-meeting ping:** one question when a meeting ends ("Anything you promised Priya?"). Answer in 5 seconds or ignore it, no guilt.
- 🎙️ **Optional 2-min brain dump** at setup: "Tell me about the 3 people you see this week."

## 🗑️ Forgetting on Purpose

- **Erase:** one memory (swipe or "Hey Altie, forget…"), a whole person, a time range, or everything.
- **Remove a person, keep the moments:** "Forget Sam, keep the memories." His name, face, contact links, tier, promises and facts about him go. What stays is YOUR experience, in your own words: what happened to you and how you handled it. Altie never adds a lesson or feeling you didn't say; it asks "What do you want to keep from this?" [💡 A lesson] [💪 How I handled it] [🗑️ Nothing]. A memory with no person and nothing kept is useless, so it goes to Recently erased. A kept lesson comes back at the next similar event ("Last land stock meetup, your note: verify investors first"). It previews every changed memory before saving.
- **Erase means everywhere:** the note, its promises and cards, search, every device, and the backup. Altie won't re-import it from Calendar or contacts.
- **Recently erased:** 30 days to change your mind, or "Erase now, forever."
- **Hide instead:** keep it but never nudge (someone who passed away, a painful time).
- **Chats with Altie:** at the end, keep 📝 summary only (default), 📜 full chat, or 🚫 nothing. Say "off the record" to skip a part, or "remember this exactly" to keep a part word for word. Altie shows the summary before saving it.

## ☝️ The Only Habit You Need

People with executive dysfunction can't keep track of everything, and that's the whole point. Twinwire asks for **one habit only: mention it to the app** ("Hey Altie, ...").

Your alter ego takes care of the rest: sorting, remembering, reminding, and following up.

## 🧩 How Altie Thinks (Architecture Principle)

- **Storage = the brain.** Everything collected day to day (mentions, events, photos, promises) lives in our own database, like personal cloud storage.
- **Search = recall.** Altie searches the brain like a web search over your own life: by person/date, or by meaning (pgvector).
- **LLM = the reasoning engine.** The model reads what was found and answers, decides, or drafts. It holds no memory itself, so models can be swapped without losing anything.
- **Links = the connections (a graph inside the database).** One `links` table (from, to, type) joins person ↔ event ↔ to-do ↔ tool ↔ lesson. Recall works in two steps: find a memory by meaning, then follow its links. v1 needs no GraphQL (that's only a way for an app to ask a server for data, and Supabase can add it later via pg_graphql) and no separate graph database such as Neo4j. Revisit only if multi-hop questions get slow.

### 📈 Built to Scale (Day-One Rules)

1. Every row has a `user_id`, locked with Supabase Row Level Security.
2. Every connector plugs into one tool layer (add new ones without rewrites).
3. One-tap export and delete-all for every user.
4. Small cheap model sorts; big model reasons. Track LLM cost per user.
5. Log every permission the user grants.

## 🔬 The Brain Model (what memory science would tell us)

**Don't copy the brain. Build a scaffold:** support the parts that fail (catching things, recalling them, remembering to act) and lean on the parts that stay strong (recognizing over recalling, routines, emotion).

| Brain part | What it does | Altie's version |
|---|---|---|
| 👀 Attention | Lets things in | Capture doors (one habit) |
| 🧮 Working memory | Holds about 4 things at once | Focus mode · one next step |
| 🗂️ Hippocampus | Binds who + where + when into one moment | The `links` table + event tags |
| 😴 Sleep | Turns the day into long-term memory | A nightly pass: summaries, links, re-index |
| 📚 Long-term memory | Moments + facts + your life story | The Wire · People · Your story |
| 🔁 Habit memory | Routines that survive memory loss | Morning brief and Plan tomorrow, same time every day |
| ⏰ Prospective memory | "Remember to do X" | Nudges tied to events, not clocks |
| ❤️ Emotion tags | Marks what matters | Priority · private feeling weather |
| 🔑 Cues | Faces, places, pictures trigger recall | Photo cards, recognition over recall |
| 🧹 Forgetting | Clears noise | Parked items · Forgetting on purpose |

**What matters for surviving long term with fading memory:** 1) identity (your story) · 2) relationships (names, shared history) · 3) routines · 4) remembering to act (meds, promises, appointments) · 5) safety (money, scams) · 6) dignity, with no shame · 7) trusting the system (always there, quick to reach, reliable) · 8) sleep, movement, social contact.

**Science rules to adopt:**
- **Recognition over recall:** show faces and choices, not blank questions.
- **Track the source:** who said it and where (people confuse sources).
- **No AI-made false memories:** keep the originals, mark AI text as a summary, and make the original one tap away. Studies show AI chats can plant false memories.
- **Optional retrieval practice:** "Who's this?" before the answer is revealed, so you keep using your own memory.
- **Measure real life:** promises kept, names recalled, days to return after a lapse.

**✅ Decided (2026-11-10):**
- 🎯 **Who:** anyone who forgets, or wants to remember better. That includes ND people (e.g. ADHD), neurotypical people, and people living with memory conditions (brain injury, early dementia).
  - **Two jobs:** 🛟 *Remember for me* (catch, sort, hand back) and 🏋️ *Help me remember* (optional practice: "Who's this?" before the answer, the warm-up deck, spaced repeats).
  - **Wording:** "practice remembering," never "improves your memory" or "prevents decline." Brain-training apps have been fined over unproven claims (FTC vs. Lumosity, 2016).
- 🩺 **Medical line:** wellness only. Altie "helps you remember and stay organized." It never diagnoses, treats or calls itself therapy, and points to 988 in a crisis. Get a regulatory check before any marketing that names a condition.
- 🔔 **Nudges:** no daily cap; the priority rules decide (see [priority-rules.md](priority-rules.md)). Cue styles rotate so nudges don't go invisible.
- 🎙️ **Other people:** your own words, plus transcripts imported from meeting tools where everyone already agreed to recording. Altie never records anyone's voice itself. For transcripts, the summary is kept by default, and they're never used in public posts.

**Still open (science angle):**
- 🟨 **Soon (moved up now that memory conditions are in):**
  - A trusted person (caregiver or partner), with your permission and clear limits on what they see
  - Choices made early: who can see what if memory gets worse, set while it's easy to decide
  - A simpler mode for bigger memory loss: fewer choices, bigger faces, the same layout every day
  - Safety: meds, money, scams
  - What happens if memory gets worse (never diagnose; suggest talking to a doctor)
- 🟨 **Soon:**
  - Balancing leaning on Altie vs. practicing your own memory
  - How the screen shows the source: "you said," "Altie summarized" or "from your calendar"
  - Painful memories (never resurface them unasked; gentle handling for people who've passed)
  - Load limits (3–4 items per screen, visual time for time blindness)
- ⬜ **Later:**
  - A pilot study with real measures (e.g. a memory questionnaire, ethics review)
  - Access needs for dyslexia, autism and low vision

## 🧠 The Core Architecture & Features

Unlike existing passive databases, Twinwire organizes your entire life into distinct life domains and handles the cognitive load for you.

- **Life-Factor Mapping:** Instead of just recording random audio, the app categorizes data into the major pillars of your life (e.g., Business/Work, Social/Networking, Health/Fitness, Daily Admin).
- **Automatic Categorized Syncing:** You do not have to sort anything manually. The app automatically detects the context of an interaction, a location, or a document, and instantly syncs it into its correct life category.
- **Neurodivergent-First Design:** Built specifically for neurodivergent individuals facing executive dysfunction, ADHD burnout, or working memory gaps. It eliminates overwhelming walls of text, replacing them with highly visual, low-friction, instantaneous context cues.
- **Response Rule (every screen, every answer):** Start with a visual cue (icon, photo, color). Very short, formatted chunks, never paragraphs. Details hide behind easy tap-tabs, one tap away, for the next thing you need.
- **Morning Brief:** Every morning, automatically: who you're meeting, what you're attending, and what to prep for each (pulled from Calendar, Jira and your mentions). Shows up when you first pick up your phone; shorter in Survival Mode.
- **Visual Cues Everywhere:** More visuals, less reading (colors, icons, progress you can see), so neurodivergent users can keep themselves on track without losing track.

## 🕹️ The Three-Mode Operating System

The app features three modes designed to take a user from a state of overwhelm to a state of absolute control. You start with Survival Mode.

**Survival → Steady → Thriving**

### 🚨 Mode 1: Survival Mode (The Immediate Clean-Up)

**The Goal:** Stop the bleeding, clear the overwhelm, and fix your day-to-day life.

- **Passive Context Anchoring:** Before you walk into a meeting or a local venue, Twinwire pushes a 3-second flashcard to your screen with just the critical details: Name, Last Context, Pending Promise.
- **Mental De-cluttering:** It acts as a shield against executive dysfunction by capturing random thoughts, untracked tasks, and forgotten names, sorting them silently in the background so your brain doesn't have to hold onto them.
- **Frictionless Recovery:** If you experience a memory lapse, a single swipe gives you an instant "cheat sheet" of your last 48 hours without forcing you to dig through massive text logs.

**Survival Tools (what a therapist would normally tell you)** — *draft list, to review with a therapist*

- **Grounding (5-4-3-2-1):** Name 5 things you see, 4 you hear, 3 you can touch... to come back to the present.
- **Box Breathing:** Breathe in 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4 to calm the panic.
- **Brain Dump:** Say everything on your mind out loud; Altie sorts it.
- **Tiniest Next Step:** Shrink any task to one small step you can do now.
- **2-Minute Rule:** If it takes under 2 minutes, do it now.
- **Body Doubling:** Altie "sits with you" while you work on a task.
- **Name It to Tame It:** Say the feeling out loud ("I'm overwhelmed") to take its power down.
- **No-Guilt Reset:** Missed something? No shame. Just pick up from now.

### ⚖️ Mode 2: Steady Mode (Holding the Ground) — *suggested, draft*

**The Goal:** Nothing is on fire anymore. Keep it that way and build consistency.

- **Autopilot Routines:** Daily basics (meds, meals, bills, replies) get gentle check-ins so they don't slip.
- **Promise Keeper:** Tracks what you said you'd do and for whom, so nothing quietly drops.
- **Ready-to-Level-Up Signal:** When you've been steady for a while, Twinwire suggests moving up to Thriving Mode. On a bad day, it can drop you back to Survival with no guilt.

### 📈 Mode 3: Thriving Mode (Prescriptive Intelligence)

**The Goal:** Move from defense to offense using forward-looking, proactive guidance.

**Who it's for:** Everyone. Not limited to neurodivergent users; neurotypical people can use Thriving Mode too.

- **Prescriptive Intelligence:** Twinwire stops just telling you what happened and starts telling you what to do next.
- **Proactive Life Management:** It analyzes the data across your major life categories to nudge you forward. For example:
  - *"You haven't followed up with the land stock contact you met 4 days ago; here is a 1-sentence draft to send now."*
  - *"Your focus has been entirely on work files for 72 hours; schedule a workout or social break today."*
- **Strategic Optimization:** It maps patterns in your energy, meetings, and habits to act as a true strategic alter ego, maximizing your confidence and performance.

## 👤 Who It's For (positioning)

**Individual-first, not corporate.** For anyone who forgets, or wants to remember better, and still wants to keep up with people (the communication game) and track what they're working on: founders, freelancers, job seekers, students, networkers, and employees using it privately.
- **Memory conditions too:** people living with brain injury or early dementia, as a wellness tool (helps you remember and stay organized), never a medical treatment.
- Corporate is later: the "bring your own Altie" path (work feeds only who + when) or approval as a workplace accommodation. A team or enterprise version would need SSO, admin controls and compliance (e.g. SOC 2), and is out of scope for now.

## 🚀 The Growth Strategy

1. **Build Just For You:** Build the initial version of Twinwire to pull yourself out of the "3-day memory fade" and streamline your own business and social circles.
2. **Target the Neurodivergent Community:** Once the tool successfully shifts your own life from Survival to Thriving mode, scale it to the millions of neurodivergent individuals who are actively looking for an external system to bypass executive dysfunction and reclaim their confidence.
