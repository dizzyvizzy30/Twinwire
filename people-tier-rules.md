# 👥 Altie's People Tier Rules

Plain-English rules Altie reads to place people in circles. Change a line here and Altie's behavior changes. No code needed.

## ⭐ Points (last 90 days, older points fade)
- 📅 Met them (Calendar): +3
- 🎙️ Mentioned them to Altie: +2
- 🤝 A promise to or from them: +2
- 💬 Shared a message or chat with them: +1

## 🪜 Tiers
- 💛 **Inner (~5):** only by my choice. Altie suggests someone after 2 months at the top of Close.
- 🤝 **Close (~15):** 20+ points, or 2+ meetings a month
- 👋 **Good (~50):** 8+ points
- 🌐 **Known (~150):** any meeting or mention in the last 6 months
- 👀 **Everyone else:** search only

Circle sizes are rough guides, never hard limits.

## 🤖 What Altie does for each tier
- 💛 **Inner:** never miss promises, birthdays, check-ins. Survival Mode still protects them.
- 🤝 **Close:** gentle nudges ("It's been 3 weeks since Sam")
- 👋 **Good:** follow-up pings after meetings
- 🌐 **Known:** a 3-second card when they show up
- 👀 **Everyone else:** find and search only

## 🏷️ Connection & Feeling (I pick, Altie never guesses)
- **Connection:** Family · Partner · Friend · Work · Mentor · Community (pick any)
- **Feeling ("relationship weather"):** ☀️ Energizing · 🌤️ Easy · ☁️ Mixed · 🌧️ Draining
- 🔒 Private: never shown on the 3-second card (someone might see my screen)

## 🗓️ Check-ins on new people (one tap, skip anytime)
- Same day: "How was Sam?" → pick a weather
- After 2 weeks, then 6 weeks, then monthly for 💛 Inner and 🤝 Close
- Shows the trend: Sam 🌤️ → ☀️
- If someone is ☀️ for 2 months, Altie asks: "Move Sam to Close?"

## 👋 Gentle reach-outs
- 1-line draft in my voice: "Hey Mom, thinking of you. Call this weekend?"
- [Open in WhatsApp] [Snooze] [Not now]. Altie never sends anything itself.
- Timed for a free gap in my Calendar, never in Survival Mode (except a tiny one for 💛 Inner)
- 🌧️ Draining people: fewer nudges, shorter suggestions
- Family: birthdays + my chosen rhythm ("Mom: every week")

## ⬆️ Moving up
- Automatic, with a small note: "Sam moved up to 🤝 Close" [Undo]

## ⬇️ Moving down (no guilt)
- Silent, one tier at a time, after 90 days with no contact
- 💛 Inner and 🤝 Close never drop silently. Altie nudges instead: "It's been 3 weeks since Sam. Want a 1-line hello?"
- 📌 Pinned people never move

## 🔁 My override always wins
- "Hey Altie, move Sam to Close", or drag his face

## 🪐 Daily people in Orbit
- 💛 Inner people (my pick) sit in the 4 corners of Orbit, outside the rings, every day, whatever the schedule. They never enter the orbit.
- Each face has a dot: 🟢 talked today (from my tap or a mention) · 🟠 something coming up or owed · no dot = quiet
- Altie can't see my calls or texts. It only knows what I tell it, my calendar, and what I send to Altie.
- Never red, and never "you haven't talked in X days"
- 🔒 **The core stays put.** Corner people change only when I change them. Altie never adds or removes anyone from the corners.
- ➕ **Add-ons, for a season:** someone becomes daily for a while (a project teammate, family visiting, a new partner, someone helping while I'm sick). They show as a small dashed face beside a corner, with an end: "until Fri," "while Mom recovers," or "no end."
  - Altie may ask once: "You've mentioned Nina 5 days in a row. Add her for now?" [➕ For now] [💛 Make core] [✖️ No]
  - When the season ends: "Nina's add-on ended. Keep her?" [💛 Core] [👋 Back to usual] (no guilt)
- 👆 **Tap any corner face** to see what Altie tracks: 🗓️ their next thing · 🤝 promises both ways · 💬 the last thing they shared · 🎂 dates · last talked. No counts, no streaks.
