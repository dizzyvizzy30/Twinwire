# 📖 Twinwire User Stories: Layer 1

**Layer 1 = take in info + sum it up + 8 specific stories.** Altie lays out the facts and you decide. No advice.
**Layer 2 (later) = prescriptive:** who to message and why, a ship ladder, LinkedIn post options, reach-out drafts, coach tools, Thriving Mode.

---

## U1 📥 Catch it

> As someone who forgets, I want to say or snap something once, so it's saved without me organizing it.

- **When:** anytime, by voice, photo, business card, text or share sheet
- **I see:** "✅ Got it" in under a second
- **Done when:**
  - [ ] Saved instantly on the phone, even offline
  - [ ] Sorted seconds later into who, what and when, then linked to the person and the event
  - [ ] One tap only if Altie is unsure ("New person: Priya Shah?")
- **New idea mid-task?** "Park it" saves the idea to 💡 Ideas, along with where you were, so your head can let go and you stay on the current task.

## U2 📝 Sum it up

> After a meeting or an event, I want a short summary, so I don't have to remember it all.

- **When:** a meeting or event ends, or a chat with Altie ends
- **I see:** 3 lines: 👤 who · 💬 what was said or learned (my words) · 🤝 what I promised
- **I can:** [✅ Keep] [✏️ Fix]
- **Done when:**
  - [ ] Saved as one bead on The Wire
  - [ ] Nothing in it that I didn't say

## U3 🌙 Plan tomorrow

> In the evening, I want to see tomorrow laid out, so the morning isn't a scramble.

- **When:** evening, 8pm by default (I pick the time). Takes about 2 minutes.
- **I see:** tomorrow as a timeline: meetings and events · promises due · what to bring or prep · clashes ("3pm overlaps")
- **I can:** move, drop or add by voice → [👍 Looks good]
- **🏋️ Brain workout (Light):** "Tomorrow you see Priya. What do you remember?" → I answer or tap Skip → then her card shows. Calm moments only.
- **Done when:**
  - [ ] Tomorrow's cards and any event pack are downloaded (for bad wifi)
  - [ ] The morning brief is built
- **Not:** advice on how to spend my day

## U4 ☀️ Morning brief

> When I wake up, I want one screen that tells me what today needs, in 30 seconds.

- **When:** first unlock after waking
- **I see:** ① the first thing · 👤 faces of who I'll see · 🤝 promises due today · 🎒 what to bring
- **Done when:**
  - [ ] Readable in 30 seconds
  - [ ] Every item taps open to its card

## U5 🎟️ Next week's events

> Once a week, I want a few local events worth my time, with honest pros and cons, so I can decide fast and let Altie register me.

- **When:** Sunday at 6pm by default, or anytime I ask "Events next week?"
- **I see:** up to 5 events near me (within 30 minutes' travel by default), matched to my interests and my story
- **Each event card:**
  - 📍 What, when, where, cost
  - ✅ **Pros:** fits my interest in AI agents · Priya is going · free · a sponsor I want to meet
  - ⚠️ **Cons:** clashes with Tue 6pm · 50 min away · $40 · ends at 10pm · similar to last week's
- **I can:** [🎟️ Register me] [🤔 Maybe] [✖️ Skip]
  - **Register me:** a quick summary → my tap → Altie fills the form in the in-app browser
  - **Maybe:** a reminder Thursday
  - **Skip:** Altie shows fewer events like it
- **Done when:**
  - [ ] Registered events land in my calendar and Events
  - [ ] Each Skip sharpens future suggestions
- **Rules:**
  - Pros and cons are facts from my calendar, story, interests and the event page, never "you should go"
  - Altie never registers without my tap and never pays
- **Sources:** event pages from Luma, Eventbrite and Meetup, found through web search

## U6 🪞 Weekly mirror

> Once a week, I want an honest, kind look at what actually moved, so I stop fooling myself and stop spinning.

- **When:** Sunday, just before Next week's events (U5). Takes about 2 minutes.
- **I see:** facts only. No score, no red, a win first.
  - ✅ What moved: "XFIX chat agent works"
  - 🧵 Projects: moving · stuck (untouched for 7+ days) · parked
  - 🗣️ Said vs. did: "You said 'finish the deck' 3 times; last touched Nov 2"
  - 📥 Took in vs. made: "4 new tools, 12 saved links · 1 thing shipped"
  - 📤 Made vs. shown: "XFIX has worked since Nov 7. Shown to: no one yet"
  - 🎯 One thing: "Day 12 of 30 · touched on 8 days" (dots, never a streak)
  - ⏱️ Guessed vs. took: "Your last 3 'quick' tasks took 3× your guess"
  - 💡 Ideas parked this week, all safe and none lost
  - 🧭 Detours from my goals: "4 this week: 2 new tools, 1 idea, 1 event." Each one is marked 🔁 back · 🅿️ parked · 🌱 growing
- **I can, for each stuck item:** [▶️ Still on] [🅿️ Park it] [✅ Call it done]
- **Done when:**
  - [ ] Every stuck item has a choice
  - [ ] Skipping a week leaves no catch-up pile
- **Rules:** never says "you procrastinated" or "you failed." No streaks. Private.

## U7 📖 Monthly look-back

> Once a month, I want to see the story my captures tell (where I drifted, what quietly became a new direction, and when), so I can find the meaning myself.

- **When:** the first Sunday of the month, about 5 minutes (or anytime: "Show me my chapters")
- **I see:**
  - 🎯 My goals this month, and how much of my time touched each
  - 🧭 Detours: how many, and which ones grew
  - 🌱 **New directions:** "'Memory apps' came up 23 times since Nov 9, and it isn't one of your goals." The **first seed** is shown, in my words, with the date
  - 📖 **Chapters:** my timeline as eras, with turning points ("LensTalk → XFIX → Twinwire, turning on Nov 9")
- **I can:**
  - For a new direction: [🎯 Make it a goal] [🔍 Just exploring] [🅿️ Park it]
  - For a chapter: Altie asks "What was this chapter about?" and my answer becomes its title
  - Pick my next 30-day one thing (U8), or keep the current one
- **Done when:**
  - [ ] Every new direction has a choice
  - [ ] Every chapter title is in my words
- **Rules:**
  - Altie shows the evidence (dates, counts, quotes) and never names the meaning for me
  - Detours are neutral, never failures
  - Private, and it can be turned off

## U8 🎯 One thing, 30 days

> I have lots of ideas and too little consistent action. I want to pick one priority for 30 days, without killing my other ideas, and put imperfect work out before I feel ready, so people can see what I can do.

- **When:** anytime ("Altie, my one thing is XFIX"), or at the Monthly look-back (U7)
- **I set, in my own words:**
  - 🎯 The one thing (for 30 days, not forever)
  - 🏁 A done line: "works for 1 real person"
  - 📤 A first show: "show 1 friend by Nov 20"
- **During the 30 days:**
  - 💡 New ideas → "Parked until Dec 12": safe, never deleted
  - 🟢 Days I touched it, shown as dots. A missed day is just blank: no streaks, no red
  - 🏁 When my done line is met: "Your done line is met." The evidence is my own words, never "you should post"
  - 📤 Shown log: every show, every ask, and any real replies
  - 💬 **Proof file:** only real words people said about my work, with who said them and when ("Priya: 'I'd use this' · Nov 7")
  - 🎲 **Asks counter:** counts asks, not yeses. A no still counts
- **Day 30, I can:** [🔁 Another 30] [🔀 Switch to a parked idea] [✅ Call it done]
- **Done when:**
  - [ ] Only one priority at a time; everything else is parked, never deleted
  - [ ] Every show and ask is logged
  - [ ] Parked ideas come back on day 30
- **Rules:**
  - Altie never picks the priority, never says "you're not ready," and never names a feeling
  - Others' words go in the Proof file only if they really said them, never paraphrased into praise
  - Layer 2 (later): 🪜 Ship ladder (show 1 friend → DM 1 person → small group → public) · post options · reach-out drafts
